What you get

European edition

Runs the EU release (CUSA14876) with the 1.21 update. The US edition keeps working too.

Online play

Online races, matchmaking, private rooms, friends list and the Pit Stop on the RyuNET server.

Discord

Your status shows the game while you play online, with join and invite support.

Unlock everything

Every character, kart and cosmetic, switchable on and off. Your saves are backed up before it turns on.

Ultrawide

Draws at your monitor's own size on wide screens, with the HUD spread to the edges.

Steam Deck

One AppImage that starts the game directly and adds itself to Steam.

Tweaks

60 FPS, no motion blur, internal resolution from 480p to 4K, button icons for Xbox, Steam Deck or Switch.

Grand Prix intros

Season start screens as they looked, with the parts 1.21 still has.

What you need

  • Your own copy of the game as decrypted files: the base game and the 1.21 update of the European or US edition.
  • Linux (any recent distribution or SteamOS) with a Vulkan 1.3 GPU, or Windows 10/11.
  • Optional: your own DLC packages (.pkg).

No game files, updates or DLC are included or downloaded. You bring your own.

Credits

A fork of Ryu7w7's OCTR-NitroFueled-PC, built on SacredFire. The game's code runs directly on the CPU, a small runtime replaces the PS4 system libraries, and a renderer derived from shadPS4 draws with Vulkan. No emulator.
