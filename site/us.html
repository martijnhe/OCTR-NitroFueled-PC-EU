What you need

  • Your own US copy as decrypted folders: the base game (CUSA13795) and the 1.21 update (often CUSA13795-patch). Or one folder with both already merged.
  • Steam Deck or Linux (x86-64, Vulkan 1.3), or Windows 10/11 (this project's Windows download is not tested yet).
  • Optional: your own US DLC packages (.pkg).

No game files, updates or DLC are included or downloaded. You bring your own.

Good to know

  • Game languages: the US build has English, Latin American Spanish, French and Brazilian Portuguese (the European build has its own set).
  • Setup: the launcher merges the base game and the update into CUSA13795-1.21; saves and the shader cache are kept under CUSA13795, apart from a European copy's.
  • Online: the same RyuNET server and nickname as on the European copy.

In the game, Insert (or L3+R3) opens the settings menu, the same as on the European copy.
