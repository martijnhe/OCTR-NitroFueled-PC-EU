European copy

The edition this project is made for. Everything on this page was developed and tested on a European copy with the 1.21 update.

What you get

  • European edition with 1.21CUSA14876 with the 1.21 update: every Grand Prix track, character and kart that came after launch.
  • UltrawideDraws at your monitor's own size; the HUD spreads to the edges; title and menu screens fill the width; videos stay 16:9.
  • Online playOnline races, matchmaking, private rooms, friends and the Pit Stop on the RyuNET server, with the EU edition's own key patch.
  • DiscordYour status shows the game while you play online, with invites that join a private room. Your nickname is linked to Discord.
  • Unlock everythingEvery character, kart and cosmetic, on and off at any time. Nothing is written to your save.
  • Steam DeckOne AppImage that sets up your game, starts it directly and adds itself to Steam for game mode.
  • Button iconsXbox, Steam Deck / Steam Controller or Switch icons in place of the PlayStation ones.
  • Grand Prix introsA season's start screen as it looked (Spooky with Nina Cortex, Rustland, Gasmoxia) and the seasons' colours.
  • Tweaks60 FPS, no motion blur, 480p to 4K, CTR TV, your own DLC, an in-game menu in English, Russian or Dutch.

What you need

  • Your own European copy as decrypted folders: the base game (CUSA14876) and the 1.21 update. Or one folder with both already merged.
  • Steam Deck or Linux (x86-64, Vulkan 1.3), or Windows 10/11 (the Windows download is not tested yet).
  • Optional: your own DLC packages (.pkg), such as the Nitros Oxide Edition and the Firehawk kart.

No game files, updates or DLC are included or downloaded. You bring your own.

Setting up

Steam Deck and Linux

  1. Download the AppImage from the releases and put it in a folder.
  2. Start it. In the launcher, choose Set up from the base game and its update: pick the base game, then the 1.21 update. They are merged into CUSA14876-1.21 next to the base game (no extra space on the same drive).
  3. Optional: DLC packages, then add your .pkg files.
  4. Press Play. From then on the AppImage starts the game directly and is in Steam, also in game mode.

Windows (untested)

  1. Download and unzip the Windows ZIP, then open CTR-NitroFueled-PC.exe.
  2. Choose the base game and the 1.21 update (or a merged folder), optionally your DLC, and press Play.

In the game, Insert (or L3+R3) opens the settings menu: ultrawide, 60 FPS, button icons, online, unlock everything and more.
