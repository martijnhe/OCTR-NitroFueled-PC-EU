## Setting up

**Steam Deck / Linux:** put the AppImage in a folder, make it executable and start it with
`--launcher` (or just start it when there is no game yet).

**Windows:** unzip and open `CTR-NitroFueled-PC.exe`: choose the base game and the 1.21 update (or a
merged folder), optionally your DLC, and press Play. The European edition (CUSA14876) is
recognised next to the US one.

In the game, **Insert** (or **L3+R3**) opens the settings menu.

## Known limitations

- The Windows ZIP is untested; the ultrawide title/customization camera and some Linux-only extras
  are not in the Windows build.
- Grand Prix events and Activision's own online services do not work (online uses RyuNET).
