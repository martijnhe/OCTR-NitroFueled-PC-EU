## Setting up

**Steam Deck / Linux:** put the AppImage in a folder, make it executable and start it with
`--launcher` (or just start it when there is no game yet):

- *Set up from the base game and its update*: pick your dumped base game, then the 1.21 update.
  They are merged next to the base game with hard links (no extra space on the same drive).
- Or pick an already merged game folder.
- *DLC packages (.pkg)*: add your own add-on packages (optional).

After that the AppImage starts the game directly and adds itself to Steam (so it works in game
mode). A merged game folder next to the AppImage is found on its own.

**Windows:** unzip and open `CTR-NitroFueled-PC.exe`: choose the base game and the 1.21 update (or a
merged folder), optionally your DLC, and press Play. The European edition (CUSA14876) is
recognised next to the US one.

In the game, **Insert** (or **L3+R3**) opens the settings menu.

## What's new in v0.5-eu.11

- **GetLostIn's launcher on Linux and the Steam Deck**: the launcher of
  [GetLostIn/CTR-NitroFueled-PC](https://github.com/GetLostIn/CTR-NitroFueled-PC) v0.4 (Play with a
  summary, Settings, Controls with a live controller test, players and button mapping, Achievements,
  Patches, Saves, DLC, Tools), brought from Windows to SDL3 and usable with a controller (full screen
  on the Deck).
- **Open the launcher at start**, a switch in the launcher and in the in-game menu (Insert or L3+R3,
  General): on, the AppImage, the Steam shortcut and the application menu's *Crash Team Racing
  Nitro-Fueled* open the launcher first; off, they start the game directly. `--launcher` always opens it.
- **21:9 and 32:9 (GetLostIn's native ultrawide, by tcosi)** now on Linux and the Steam Deck too:
  menus, backgrounds and a centered HUD made for 21:9 (2560x1080, 3440x1440) and 32:9 (5120x1440),
  for the 1.21 update of the European and USA editions, **online too**. One choice, *Aspect / output*
  (launcher: Settings > Display; in-game menu: Picture). The first start in 21:9 or 32:9 prepares a
  patched copy of the changed archives once (about 9 GB; the rest are hard links to your game, which is
  not changed); upscaling is off with it. Play once in 16:9 to the main menu first.
- **Internal resolution above 1440p with ultrawide**: 1620p and 4K work with 21:9 and 32:9 (21:9 at
  4K renders 5160x2160 and is scaled down to your screen).
- From upstream v0.5.4 (Ryu7w7): older CPUs without MOVBE/BMI1/LZCNT, achievements with notices in the
  game, the in-game menu in tabs and in Spanish (Dutch stays), every race track in the online playlist,
  the shader recipe.

Everything since the fork: [Versions of this edition](https://github.com/martijnhe/OCTR-NitroFueled-PC-EU#versions-of-this-edition-eu)
(EU edition with 1.21, 60 FPS, no motion blur, ultrawide, unlock everything, online on RyuNET,
Discord, CTR TV, Steam shortcuts, DLC).

## Known limitations

- The Windows ZIP is untested; the new launcher and some Linux-only extras are not in the Windows
  build (it keeps the upstream launcher).
- Grand Prix events and Activision's own online services do not work (online uses RyuNET).
- With the 60 FPS patch, races reach about 37-45 FPS on the Steam Deck.
